# Writing for mindspan.co/learn

Posts live in `content/learn/`. Authors live in `content/authors/`. Both are plain markdown with a short header. You do not need to touch these files by hand. Use the editor.

## How to publish a post

1. Open https://mindspan.co/admin and sign in with GitHub.
2. Choose **Posts**, then **New Post**.
3. Fill in the title, summary, date, author, tags and a hero image. Write the body in the editor.
4. Click **Save**. The post is now a draft. Nobody can see it on the site.
5. Move the post to **In review** from the Workflow tab when it is ready.
6. A reviewer reads it, checks the preview link, and publishes it. The site rebuilds and the post goes live within a few minutes.

You cannot publish your own post. A reviewer must approve it first. That is intended.

## Rules for every post

- No em dashes. Use a comma, a period or parentheses.
- Do not describe a real patient in a way that could identify them. Change names, places and details, or write in general terms.
- Do not promise outcomes or times to results ("diagnosis in two weeks", "slows decline by 30%"). Describe what we do and what is known.
- Link to sources for medical claims.
- Images: 1600 by 900 pixels, JPEG or WebP, under 400 KB. Describe the image in the alt text field.
- The site is public, and so is the GitHub repository behind it. Drafts are visible there before they are published. Do not draft anything confidential.

## Adding a Mindspan film

Paste the film's address on a line of its own, with a blank line above and below:

```
https://mindspan.co/watch/first-visit
```

On the site that line becomes a video player with the film's poster. The same address inside a sentence stays an ordinary link. Films that exist today: `first-visit`, `care-plan` and `referral`. Ask the web team to add a new film before linking it, because an unknown film stops the site from building.

## Authors

Add yourself once under **Authors**. Use your name, your role, and two or three sentences about you. A reviewer approves author entries the same way as posts.

## Voice

Write like an experienced clinician talking to a worried family in a waiting room. Short sentences. Plain words. Specific and kind. The Marketing Brain in Notion has the full voice guide.
